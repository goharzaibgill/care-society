FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about volunteering

Who can volunteer?

Any student enrolled at the university can volunteer. No prior experience is required – we welcome everyone with enthusiasm and a willingness to help!

How much time do I need to commit?

We offer flexible volunteering opportunities. You can participate in one-time events or commit to regular weekly activities based on your schedule.

What kind of activities will I do?

Activities include organizing charity events, blood donation camps, student support programs, community outreach, and awareness campaigns.

Still Have Questions?

Contact us directly and we'll be happy to help you get started on your volunteering journey.
